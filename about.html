Welcome! I'm delighted to share a little about who I am. By profession, I am deeply engaged in the world of research and product development. I thrive on the intellectual challenges, the process of inquiry, and the satisfaction of contributing meaningful work to my field.

My core belief is that in our rapidly digitalized world, we must find better ways to interact with technology. My mission is to focus on building genuine products that people can truly use and seamlessly integrate as a valuable part of their daily lives.

While research sharpens my mind and focuses my professional purpose, I find immense joy in creative and outdoor pursuits. I recharge by turning to embroidery and painting for artistic expression, cooking and gardening for hands-on creation, and hiking and reading for exploration and perspective.

I look forward to connecting! Email me or book an appointment.
